# About BuildLab

BuildLab is a student Software Engineering community focused on learning, collaboration, and building real-world software projects.

Our goal is to help members improve their technical skills, learn professional development practices, and gain experience working together as a team.

We learn by doing. We research problems, design solutions, write code, test our work, and learn from feedback.

At BuildLab, we believe that strong engineers are developed through consistent learning, practical experience, teamwork, and accountability.
